# Why this target

Checked 2026-10-21.

- **The incumbent.** LandTracker, from LandTrack Systems, now part of Datamine's exploration portfolio, describes itself as a multi-jurisdictional asset and compliance management system for tenements, agreements and environmental conditions, audited against government databases. Expenditure Watch, GeoSearch and Tenement Safety Net are sold alongside it. The product page (https://www.dataminesoftware.com/landtracker/) offers a contact form and an expert session; no price is published.
- **The rules that make every explorer need a register.** In Western Australia, a Form 5 within 60 days of every anniversary, rent within one month of it, an exemption application within 60 days when the commitment is missed, a 40 per cent compulsory partial surrender at the end of year six, and an extension applied for before expiry. On ASX, a tenement schedule every quarter under Listing Rule 5.3.3.
- **Who buys.** ASX-listed and private junior explorers, and the tenement management consultancies in Perth and Kalgoorlie that run registers for them.
- **Rivals seen.** PX4 Resources, Global Tenements (consultancy with software), K2fly, and consultancies running spreadsheets. None publishes a price.
- **Import path.** A tenement schedule saved as CSV from the incumbent's report, a consultant's spreadsheet or the department's register search, and expenditure lines from the accounts.
- **Out of scope.** Maps and boundaries, automatic checks against the government register, lodging with the department, paying rent, releasing to ASX, other states' rules (added with `/customise`).
